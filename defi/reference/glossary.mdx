---
title: "Glossary"
---

Terms used across Palliora DeFi, in alphabetical order. Each links to the page that explains it.

**Accountable privacy.** Palliora's model for private systems. A transaction is private by default, and it can be de-anonymized when there is a real reason, through due process and never through a master key. See [Accountable privacy and disclosure](/defi/learn/concepts/accountable-privacy-and-disclosure).

**Adaptor.** A small contract between the pool and another protocol. It lets a private transaction swap, lend or stake. See [Adaptors](/defi/learn/concepts/adaptors).

**Address tree.** The Merkle tree of registered root addresses. Its depth is 20. See [Notes and commitments](/defi/learn/concepts/notes-and-commitments).

**Asset id.** The number the pool gives each registered token. Ids are specific to a pool. See [Network reference](/defi/reference/network-reference).

**Blinded address.** The hash of a root address, the revoker's public key and a fresh random number. A note carries a blinded address and not your root address. See [Shielded accounts](/defi/learn/concepts/shielded-accounts).

**Bundler.** A service that collects ERC-4337 user operations and submits them to Ethereum. See [Relaying and gas](/defi/learn/concepts/relaying-and-gas).

**Commitment.** A hash of a note that goes on the public record. The note itself stays private. See [Notes and commitments](/defi/learn/concepts/notes-and-commitments).

**Commitment tree.** The Merkle tree of all commitments. Its depth is 25.

**Compute Contract.** A Palliora contract that coordinates storing and retrieving a report for the authorized actors. See [Palliora integration](/defi/learn/concepts/palliora-integration).

**Data Availability.** Palliora's layer that keeps data available and shows if it was altered. The revoker's report is stored on it, and this page calls it Palliora DA. See [Palliora integration](/defi/learn/concepts/palliora-integration).

**Decom guardian.** A node that holds one share of the disclosure key and answers valid requests with a partial decryption. It is not a Palliora Guardian. See [Accountable privacy and disclosure](/defi/learn/concepts/accountable-privacy-and-disclosure).

**De-anonymize.** To recover the notes a transaction created and who sent it. The revoker does it together with a threshold of Decom guardians.

**Disclosure.** Showing what a private transaction did, either by the revoker and the Decom guardians together or by you giving a view key to someone you choose.

**Disclosure key.** The public key every transaction's seed is encrypted to. It is the guardians' shared secret times the generator, plus the revoker's public key.

**DKG.** Distributed key generation. The way the guardians make their shared secret without any one of them holding the whole of it. See [Guardian key operations](/defi/participate/guardian-key-operations).

**EntryPoint.** The standard ERC-4337 contract that runs user operations. The pool uses version 0.7.

**Funding adaptor.** The adaptor that moves USDC from the pool to a Private Sub-account on HyperEVM. See [Private Sub-accounts](/defi/learn/concepts/private-sub-accounts).

**GasStation.** A contract that sells a fixed amount of HYPE for a fixed amount of USDC, so a Private Sub-account can pay gas to return. See [Fees](/defi/reference/fees).

**Gateway.** The contract that acts as the account for an ERC-4337 user operation that wraps a shielded transaction. See [Relaying and gas](/defi/learn/concepts/relaying-and-gas).

**Groth16.** The proof system Palliora DeFi uses. See [Cryptographic primitives](/defi/learn/concepts/cryptographic-primitives).

**Note.** A piece of value of one token that belongs to one account. You spend a whole note and may get change back. See [Notes and commitments](/defi/learn/concepts/notes-and-commitments).

**Nullifier.** A value revealed when a note is spent. The pool rejects a second transaction that reveals the same one, so a note cannot be spent twice.

**Palliora Core.** Palliora's privacy engine. Palliora DeFi uses it to keep the revoker's report. See [Palliora integration](/defi/learn/concepts/palliora-integration).

**Palliora Guardian.** A node in Palliora's silent threshold encryption layer. It holds a share of the key that protects a stored report. It is not a Decom guardian.

**Paymaster.** A contract that pays the gas on Ethereum and takes its fee from your notes. Its address is bound into the proof. See [Fees](/defi/reference/fees).

**Pool.** The shielded pool contract. It holds the tokens, records commitments and nullifiers, and checks proofs. There is one on Ethereum and one on HyperEVM. See [Network reference](/defi/reference/network-reference).

**Poseidon.** The hash function used inside the circuits.

**Private Sub-account.** A fresh Hyperliquid trading address that you fund from your shielded balance and bring back. See [Private Sub-accounts](/defi/learn/concepts/private-sub-accounts).

**Registration.** The public step that links your wallet address to your shielded address, so people can pay you. See [Shielded accounts](/defi/learn/concepts/shielded-accounts).

**Relayer.** A service that submits a proven transaction and pays the gas. On HyperEVM it calls the pool directly. See [Relayer API](/defi/build/relayer-api).

**Report.** The encrypted record a revoker writes to Palliora DA after it de-anonymizes a transaction. Only the revoker, or any third party the revoker grants access to, can read it. See [Palliora integration](/defi/learn/concepts/palliora-integration).

**Revoker.** The party that asks to de-anonymize a transaction. It holds a private key that is one half of the disclosure key. The pool owner registers each revoker.

**Root address.** A hash of your sign public key and your view private key. It is the identity your notes belong to.

**Settling.** The step where queued commitments join the commitment tree, through a tree update. A note can be spent only after it is settled.

**Shielded account.** Your private account in the pool. Your wallet signature recreates it.

**Shielded address.** The 96-byte address people use to send you private payments.

**Sign key.** The key pair that authorizes spending. Keep the private key secret.

**Silent threshold encryption.** The scheme Palliora uses to encrypt a stored report to a group of Palliora Guardians, with no interaction between them.

**Staleness limit.** How old a price feed may be before the pool refuses deposits of that asset. See [Status and limits](/defi/reference/status-and-limits).

**Tree update.** A transaction with a proof that adds queued commitments to the commitment tree. Anyone can submit it.

**View key.** The key pair that lets you read your balance and history. Sharing the private key shows everything, and it cannot spend.

**ZK ceremony.** The multi-party setup that made the proving and verification keys. See [ZK ceremony](/defi/reference/zk-ceremony).
