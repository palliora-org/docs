Palliora DeFi has a hosted demo app on Manas, Palliora's public testnet. It shows what an integration looks like. Production use goes through the SDK, see [Palliora DeFi](/products/palliora-defi).

The demo is at app.veilnyx.com for now, and the app may still show its former name, Veilnyx. The URL will change.

| What you can try                                                        | Link                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Create a private account, deposit test tokens, send a shielded transfer | [app.veilnyx.com/discover](https://app.veilnyx.com/discover) |

This walks through connecting a wallet, creating a private account, depositing tokens, and sending a shielded transfer. The demo covers transfers only. Palliora DeFi itself is live on testnet on Hyperliquid and Ethereum, see [Palliora DeFi](/products/palliora-defi).

### Set up your account

1. **Open the app.** Go to [app.veilnyx.com/discover](https://app.veilnyx.com/discover) to see all supported applications and protocols.
2. **Connect your wallet.** On the Home tab, first-time users connect a non-custodial wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, or similar).
3. **Register your wallet.** Once connected, a Register modal appears. Click **Register** to link your wallet to Palliora DeFi.
4. **Generate your shielded account.** You'll be asked to sign a wallet message, an app-specific signature, not a transaction, that generates your unique shielded account. Every private transaction needs this.
5. **Review how disclosure works.** Before you use your shielded account, the app asks you to confirm how disclosure works. Your activity stays private. It can only be revealed to answer a signed, publicly logged request approved by independent Guardians, and only the record that request covers. See [Accountable privacy](/learn/concepts/accountable-privacy).

### Deposit tokens

6. **Deposit tokens.** Once your shielded account exists, deposit tokens from your regular wallet (EOA) into it. This moves your funds into the privacy layer.
7. **Wait for the balance to settle.** A deposited balance may sit in a "Pending" state before it's usable. It moves to "Spending" (or "Available") either automatically after a short period, or when you trigger a settle-balance action in the app.

### Send privately

8. **Send.** Once your balance is in the Spending state, you can send tokens privately. The recipient also needs their own registered shielded account to receive a private transfer.

### What the world sees

The transfer is recorded publicly. Who sent it, who received it, and their balances stay private. They can be revealed only through the due process described in [Accountable privacy](/learn/concepts/accountable-privacy). No single party, not the revoker and not any one Guardian, can unmask anyone on its own.
