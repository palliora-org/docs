---
title: "Get Test Tokens"
---

How to claim PALI on the testnet faucet so you can start using Palliora.

Before you begin, make sure you're [logged in](/use/account-management) with a wallet or an email address.

1. **Open the faucet.** In the app, click **Faucet**.
2. **Complete security verification.** Follow the on-screen verification process.
3. **Claim tokens.** Click **Claim tokens**. A confirmation message appears once the request succeeds.
4. **Verify your balance.** Check your balance in the app, it should update shortly after the claim completes.
