title: "What You Can Build"
---

Palliora DeFi and Palliora AI show the pattern. Anything that needs private data with a public guarantee can be built on Compute Contracts.

| Area          | Example                                     | What Palliora adds                                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Finance       | Private credit scoring                      | Lenders check creditworthiness without seeing raw financial data            |
| Finance       | Sealed-bid auctions and private order books | Bids stay hidden until the rules say otherwise, and the outcome is provable |
| AI            | Selling access to a dataset or model        | Buyers pay to compute on it without ever receiving it                       |
| AI            | Training on personal data                   | Contributors stay private and are paid automatically                        |
| Data          | Ad matching and attribution                 | Match audiences across companies without sharing user lists                 |
| Health        | Multi-party research                        | Run studies across institutions without moving patient data                 |
| Public sector | Citizen-data processing                     | Process sensitive records under strict, auditable access rules              |
