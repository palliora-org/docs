Palliora starts with finance and AI. Its first two products are built in-house, as proof of what Palliora can do and as SDKs for the businesses that own the user relationship: exchanges, brokers, wallets, trading front ends, AI platforms and model routers.

| At a glance       | Palliora DeFi                                                                                                                              | Palliora AI                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tagline           | Privacy for traders. Answers for regulators.                                                                                               | Private data. Accountable agents.                                                                                                       |
| What it does      | Keeps traders' positions, history and strategy private; never takes custody of assets                                                      | Runs models on private data in a TEE, with a record of what ran                                                                         |
| Who integrates it | Exchanges, brokers, wallets, trading front ends, institutions                                                                              | Model routers, AI platforms, agent builders, enterprise AI teams                                                                        |
| Pricing           | None active on the live deployments                                                                                                        | To be announced                                                                                                                         |
| Status            | Live on Ethereum and HyperEVM mainnet, and on two testnets; private trading on Hyperliquid through Private Sub-accounts; vaults on testnet | SDK in development on testnet; demo app (AI Workbench) live at [ai.palliora.org](https://ai.palliora.org/); router plug-ins in progress |
| Docs              | [Palliora DeFi](/products/palliora-defi), and the [full documentation](/defi)               | [Palliora AI](/products/palliora-ai)                                                                                                 |

Customers pay in fiat or stablecoins. PALI works in the background.

The hosted apps are demos. Production use goes through the SDKs. Anything else that needs private data with a public guarantee can be built the same way, see [What you can build](/products/use-cases).
